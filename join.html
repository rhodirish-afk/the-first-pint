Come in during Cask Ale Week — or any week after — and ask for a first-pint third. We will pour whatever is in best condition that day.

Who this is for

  • First-timers and 18–30s — walk in, ask for a third, no lecture.
  • Publicans — keep one cask well, chalk up “first pint? ask for a third.”
  • CAMRA branches — help list the pubs that already do it properly.
  • Brewers, students, clubs — run it in a taproom, freshers’ week, or anywhere cask went quiet.
