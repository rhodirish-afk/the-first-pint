Come in during Cask Ale Week — or any week after that — and ask for a first-pint third. We will pour whatever is in best condition that day and tell you what it is without a sermon.

Who this is for

  • Anyone who has never tried it — walk into a pub that keeps it well and ask for a third of whatever is on the hand pump.
  • Publicans — keep one beer in genuinely good condition and chalk up “first pint? ask for a third.”
  • CAMRA branches — help us find the pubs that already keep cask well.
  • Brewers, students, clubs — run it in a taproom, a freshers’ week or a members’ club that gave up on cask years ago.
