What we are asking CAMRA

Not a rebrand. Not a festival takeover. Work with us, not around us.

Help us find the pubs that already keep cask well, and ask them to pour a third for anyone who has never tried it.

Branch chairs and Good Beer Guide compilers already know those pubs. That is the foundation. A list of places that will write “first pint? ask for a third” on a chalkboard during Cask Ale Week — and keep doing it after the banners come down.

Oxfordshire first, because that is where The Vine is. Then any branch that wants the same thing for their own patch.

Why together

CAMRA has the branches, the festivals and the Good Beer Guide. We have the pubs, the hand pumps and the first conversation at the bar. Between those two things there is a generation that has never been asked.

CAMRA’s 2026–29 strategy already talks about new audiences. There is a Young Members group for 18–30s and cheaper membership under 26. This campaign is meant to sit alongside that work — not compete with it.
